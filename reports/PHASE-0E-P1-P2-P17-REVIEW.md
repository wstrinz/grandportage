## P17 — Magma / PARI rigor and corrected A27

**Paper:** Elsenhans–Voight, author PDF dated by filename 02Oct25; [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05501v1) submitted **2025-10-07**, describing Magma V2.29. It distinguishes five regimes: formalizable, rigorous, GRH-conditional versions of each, and heuristic. Formalizable permits a future proof-assistant check, with ball arithmetic if needed; rigorous allows its stated floating error-analysis treatment. “One step beyond formalizable would be formalized.” Default class group is described as formalizable; default units as rigorous. V2.29 repairs missing class saturation and nonrigorous GRH Euler-product evaluation in earlier versions. Version and successful computation matter; a regime label is not a formal proof artifact. [P17-PAPER](https://jvoight.github.io/articles/clgpunitgp-magma-02Oct25.pdf).

**Official Magma handbook:** V2.29, **3 August 2026**. ClassGroup defaults to Full with a Minkowski bound, but Full follows global settings: after `SetClassGroupBounds("GRH")` it is conditional. `Proof:="GRH"` and `"Bound"` are conditional; explicit Bound alone defaults to Subgroup. Current repeats prior options and results are cached. The handbook's old comparison with PARI does not override PARI's own current manual. [P17-CLASS](https://magma.maths.usyd.edu.au/magma/handbook/text/416).

UnitGroup normally performs its own proof phase, even after heuristic class-group work, and does not thereby prove the class group. `GRH:=true` skips that phase and inherits the class computation's rigor. IndependentUnits yields a finite-index subgroup; full-group correctness depends on the class computation. `SetOrderUnitsAreFundamental` is an assumption-setting operation, not certification evidence. [P17-UNIT](https://magma.maths.usyd.edu.au/magma/handbook/text/417).

**Official PARI stable manual:** “The result is conditional on the GRH”. Initial `bnfinit` bounds are increased until generation is justified under GRH; small initial bounds do not establish current heuristic defaults. Flag 1 retains exact data and units; it does **not** remove GRH. `bnfcertify(bnf,0)=1`, with fundamental units available, supports full certification; an incorrect object may error or fail to terminate. Flag 1 certifies only a class-group quotient / unit subgroup relation. Even an unconditional generator bound alone does not fully certify the bnf. [P17-PARI](https://pari.math.u-bordeaux.fr/dochtml/html-stable/General_number_fields.html). The “Bach constant cheating” account is historical December 2003. [P17-HISTORY](https://pari.math.u-bordeaux.fr/archives/pari-users-0312/msg00020.html). The download page lists stable **2.19.0, 2026-09-28**; the moving manual was not independently byte-bound to that release. [P17-PARI-RELEASE](https://pari.math.u-bordeaux.fr/download.html).

| Evidence situation | Proposed GP treatment |
|---|---|
| Heuristic class/full-unit claim | Record diagnostic evidence; no unconditional held promotion. Pin historical example to a release. |
| GRH-conditional result | Include the precisely required GRH hypothesis in scope; admit only a checker rule that establishes this claim under it. |
| Full successful certification | Candidate for unconditional scope only after exact input/result binding and separate GP checker admission. |
| Partial subgroup/quotient certification | Preserve that weaker proposition; refuse promotion to equality/full group. |
| Formalizable / rigorous algorithm description | Record assurance mechanism and numerical obligations; neither is an executed GP receipt or formalized theorem. |

**A27 evidence proposal, no cases or expectations changed:** bind the defining polynomial/order, requested proposition, tool version, effective bound source/value, flags, cached history, relevant precision/units and successful check to one result. Distinguish exact representation, generation/completeness justification and checker admission. A positive control needs bound input/configuration, successful certification and admitted authority. Preserve negative controls for GRH removal, partial-to-full promotion, label-only promotion and unrelated certification substitution. Number-theory implementation is not required in this review.
