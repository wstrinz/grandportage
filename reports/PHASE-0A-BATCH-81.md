The finite classifier promotes a supplied sufficient rule upward to supersets of premises and a supplied countermodel downward to subsets it satisfies. It rejects simultaneous positive and negative matches, retains inclusion-minimal alternatives among supplied rules, and leaves other cells UNKNOWN. This is catalog reasoning, not theorem discovery or global weakest-premise inference.
