# Phase 0a - formalization ledger extraction

Verified here: 103 neutral cases (29 acceptance controls, 74 refusals). Replay returned 96 agreements, four known conservative differences, one diagnostic observation, one pending case and one unsupported case. Existing cases and expected verdicts were not changed.

## What the ledger actually checks

GP-X51–59 extract all nine historical mathematical scenarios: forward forgetting of provenance, unjustified reverse realization, codomain-based lower-bound credit, finite-to-formal and initial-to-actual lifts, field widening, deleted unit-coordinate displacements, slice-to-full-tower reversal and predicate widening to a relaxed region.

Six routes call the pinned kernel directly, without consulting the historical fixture's expected-verdict labels. They are conditional transport-table observations, assuming the declared relation prerequisites. Two routes report the historical out-of-vocabulary guard. GP-X53 is explicitly UNSUPPORTED: the old system cannot express dimension/rank-credit claims. Its must-refuse expectation remains, but there is no observed mathematical refusal to count as an agreement.

GP-X60–62 check the conditional block-one theorem's endpoint and premise bindings. Retaining the remainder bound, slice-solution assumption and all-orders gauge preserves the metadata; dropping the remainder bound or rebinding the target fails. The normalizer runs before any expected-answer comparison. None of these results discharges the premises or grants theorem authority.

## A regression diagnostic is not an authority check

Two old mutation tests fail with ASY8: changing a forgetting relation to equivalence, or changing a field-relative assay's scope to SCHEME. Direct inspection and executable calibration show why: the conditional transport table accepts the relabeled input, then the ledger notices disagreement with its stored expected answer.

In-memory copies with those test labels matched to the computed result compile again. The resulting ledger still has DERIVED_READ_MODEL_ONLY authority and graph_effect NONE. This is not a reproduced false held claim or a new v0.37 soundness finding. It demonstrates why an expected-answer regression must not be presented as a proof-admission guard. The audit is reproducible with tools/audit-formalization-ledger.py and recorded in FORMALIZATION-LABEL-AUDIT.json. No saved corpus expectation or frozen fixture was changed.
