## Reading the judgments

True means a concrete recorded attempted authority/reach/binding failure maps to the proposed K1–K5 interface, under the row's explicit faithful adapter/profile and admitted-soundness conditions. It is a conditional architectural paper judgment, never a runtime catch, historical verdict or implemented domain checker. False means the recorded mechanism lies outside the core guarantee or concerns intended relevance/meaning despite a potentially correct weak claim. Null means no supported whole-row conclusion follows without an unspecified domain, decoder, lifecycle or adapter contract. Null is not false and does not mean evidence is absent.

Representable custody is narrower than domain validation. A hash can bind an incorrect matrix, shadowing program or lossy encoding consistently. A generic Stmt capable of representing arbitrary propositions earns no positive score here. Class labels are eligibility inputs, not automatic catch diagnoses. Source correction/group allegations remain attributed to retained records; no primary papers, old code, publication artifacts or campaign outputs were reopened.

The JSON contains exact input hashes, the complete finite eligibility lists with class pointers, 104 individual judgments with /claimed, /problem, /notes and /cost pointers, qualifications, and the 11 excluded MEANING owners. All manifest-listed file hashes were checked without executing their contents.

## Specification gaps

- **GAP-01: warrant/lifecycle.** No typed event constructors/decoder, warrant IDs, exact retraction targets, supersession state, dependency graph, acyclicity/live support predicate or ordered fold is defined. Multiple-support survival and failed-retry behavior are proposals; soundness obligation does not prove them. Reference: SPEC-CORE.md Held, K6; Log.eventBytes.
- **GAP-02: binding.** Strings are not a defined encoding relation. Need explicit current statement/scope/model/input/version identity, faithful byte/canonicalization contract, and binding-to-receipt correspondence. CheckerSound establishes Means at checker reach if valid, but does not prove Binding content describes current objects. Reference: Binding; Checker.valid; CheckerSound.
- **GAP-03: scope.** K2 direction is sound universal narrowing. No concrete field, point/embedding, region, characteristic or clean-completion profile is supplied. Cannot infer mathematical transport from typeclass labels; conservative profile tests may refuse valid transports. Empty-scope truth is vacuous unless profiles/checks expose reach/nonemptiness as required. Reference: Profile.mem/le/le_sound; Means.
- **GAP-04: coverage.** Search-universe coverage, partitions, orbit equivalence, stopping certificates and exhaustive negative/count routes are not represented concretely. Finite closure of admitted rules does not imply exhaustive campaign search. Reference: SPEC-CORE.md finite materialization domain; Rule.sideValid.
- **GAP-05: earned closure.** Finite materialization domain, saturation algorithm, rule instantiation and termination/completeness predicate absent. heldSoundStatement only promises truth of positive held answers; it permits always-false held and makes no earned-but-unclaimed guarantee. Reference: Log; ImplementationSlot; heldSoundStatement.
- **GAP-06: inconsistency.** No overlap test, witness/proof of overlapping scopes, unresolved-overlap finding or promotion freeze state is formalized. contra_sound alone does not implement conflict discovery or freeze. Reference: Profile.contra/contra_sound; SPEC-CORE.md Inconsistency.
- **GAP-07: checker/proof admission.** Contracts are assumptions awaiting concrete admission with soundness argument and adversarial controls. No receipt language/domain checker or bound Lean theorem/axiom/hypothesis-to-scope route is instantiated here; neither labels nor missing dependencies prove an adapter output wrong. Reference: CheckerSound; RuleSound; G1 proposals.
- **GAP-08: surfaces/host.** Sound held claims do not guarantee error containment, availability, filesystem ownership, transaction acknowledgments, render fidelity, diagnostic health, canonical lexical schema, intended meaning or bibliography/source interpretation. Reference: ImplementationSlot versus operational records.

The small theorem weakening_sound has the correct universal implication direction. heldSoundStatement is a goal for the actual future fold, not a theorem about an implemented fold. It does not establish lifecycle, consistency finding, bound-proof admission, decoder correctness or earned closure; an always-false held predicate would satisfy soundness while exposing no consequences.

## Earned but unclaimed

A sound registered rule and finite declared materialization domain can expose useful narrowing, explicit joins/transports and consequences of live supporting warrants that prose has not claimed. It can retain an independent success when another support is withdrawn and leave missing premises as obligations. The mechanism helps only where actual admitted evidence/rules earn those consequences.

It cannot discover the intended objective, lift, threshold, diagram or source attribution; repair tautological checks, variable shadowing, canonicalization, heuristic admissibility or lost witnesses; produce a missing transport/coverage theorem; authorize a checker implicitly; or widen scope via K2. Finite rule closure is not completeness of a search universe or of all mathematical consequences. Earned closure remains a proposal pending GAP-01 and GAP-05.
