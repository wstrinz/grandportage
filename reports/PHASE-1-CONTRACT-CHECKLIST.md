# Phase 1 contract checklist — proposals for G1 review
This supplements the one-page SPEC-CORE.md without implementing Phase 2 or admitting a checker. The typed target elaborates; the actual executable fold and its proofs remain future work.

**Separate obligations.** heldSoundStatement establishes truth of positive held answers under CheckerSound and RuleSound. It does not establish freshness, deterministic folding, parser conservation, completeness of earned closure, support survival, conflict discovery or availability. Phase 2 must connect the target to its actual functions and verify the other specified properties separately. An always-false held predicate cannot satisfy the useful earned-closure contract even though it satisfies soundness.

**Identity and decoding.** Proposed boundary: canonical typed statement/scope/model records plus immutable input snapshots and authority versions. Reject unknown event constructors/status values as malformed input; a failed, truncated, absent or cited attempt never receives checked-success authority. Do not promote generic tool labels. Hashes bind exact recorded bytes, not their intended mathematical meaning. Digest algorithm, canonicalization and validation-to-use snapshot contracts must be explicit. Where custody relies only on digests, collision/authenticity assumptions belong in the TCB; they do not follow from string equality. A checker must validate the actual proposed statement/model, not merely its displayed ID. Evidence: ARR-O08, DK-B007/B009, JC-B007/B011 and supplemental GP-OP-74.3/74.5/76.7.

**Warrant lifecycle.** Proposed finite typed events register a claim and immutable warrant, set a current input/authority version, and retract a named warrant. Conflicting reuse of an ID is malformed; no silent replacement. Current bindings are resolved by explicit ordered events; merge remains ordered concatenation and re-fold, not commutative conflict resolution. Derived warrants reference specific supporting warrant IDs. Require well-founded support; circular support cannot bootstrap authority. Recompute live support after retraction or supersession; retain all historical records. A rejected retry adds no authority and does not erase independent support. This is an acceptance contract, not a proved fold. Evidence: DK-B025, GP-SRC-DK-GP03 and GP-SRC-CQ-G02.

**Finite earned closure.** Proposed materialization domain consists of finitely declared claim keys, narrowing requests and admitted rule instances. Compute the least reachable support closure over that domain from current validated warrants, without cyclic self-support. Earned means reachable under those instances even if no campaign assertion named the conclusion. Adding a new mathematical rule or claim domain is explicit; no algorithm discovers all logical consequences. A future completion theorem/property compares computed support with that finite reachability relation. Enumeration of campaign objects, orbit coverage and optimality remain separate profile statements and checked evidence. Evidence: DK-B001/B021, PR-C04/C05 and JC-C003.

**Scope and contradiction.** K2 universally narrows ambient assumptions; it never changes the model, chosen branch, search region or selected point. Object-changing maps require sound K3 premises. Scopes may be empty; truth there is vacuous and supplies no witness of existence. If an operation needs an inhabited scope, require that evidence separately. A conflict requires contradictory statements and proved inhabited overlap of their scopes. Unknown overlap produces an unresolved finding. Proposed freeze prevents new promotions while retaining evidence and the exact conflicting supports; it is not a proof that previously held claims were false. Soundness under admitted contracts and operational conflict handling are different obligations. Evidence: JC-B003, JC-C001, DK-B027 and A05/A08 b.

**Host limits.** The above contracts do not guarantee a reviewer reads current prose, faithful rendering, filesystem permissions, service survival or intended relevance of an accurately encoded weak/tautological claim. Preserve those cases and assign their verification to adapters/surfaces; do not count them as runtime kernel catches. Evidence: JC-B005/B008/B009 and supplemental GP-OP-58.2/63.2/63.3.

These are bounded design recommendations for review. No decoder, fold, production profile, receipt checker or adoption has been implemented by this checklist.
