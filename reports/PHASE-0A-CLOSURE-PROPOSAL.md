## What completion means

Phase 0a delivers a source-complete, neutral regression corpus of GP's recorded counterexamples, traps, confessions and regressions, with positive controls and honest oracle observations. It does not certify the predecessor's entire implementation or settle the new kernel design.

Packet basis: section 6, Phase 0a items 1-6 and G0. Completeness is over incidents in the source/history boundary, not a numerical case target. Repeated occurrences may share a case only when the attempted conclusion, relevant premises and failure mechanism match; record every occurrence's pointer. Shared subject matter alone is insufficient.

## Proposed exit checklist

1. **Source boundary accounted for.** Reconcile the 368 pinned-tree files, 244 indexed history commits and retained historical/deleted material. Check whether the index actually covers the intended reachable history and relevant deleted paths; do not assume its size proves completeness. Every source/history group has a review disposition and evidence pointer. Duplicate merge/release material may reference the content review that subsumes it. No keyword-only exclusion and no silent missing source.
2. **Every identified incident resolved for extraction.** An incident maps to existing neutral case IDs, newly extracted cases, or a specific reason it is not a counterexample/trap/confession/regression. A missing fixture, unclear expected verdict or unreviewed candidate remains open. Operational regressions are not excluded merely because they do not concern mathematical truth: preserve the relevant observable contract and distinguish it from held authority.
3. **Cases are sufficient and neutral.** Each case supplies the situation, attempted conclusion, expected verdict/reason and stable source pointer. Inputs are adequate to test the intended distinction without treating predecessor fields as the new spec. Each distinct refusal mechanism has an appropriate positive control, or a written reason a paired control is inapplicable. Audit families rather than requiring an arbitrary one-to-one pairing.
4. **Oracle evidence is complete and honestly classified.** Every case has a pinned, reproducible observation or an explicit supported explanation of why the oracle cannot express/execute it. Separate rule-level, arithmetic, diagnostic and persisted-authority observations. No unexplained errors, missing routes or pending fixtures at a strict closeout. Differences from fixed expectations are findings, not automatic blockers. Unsupported/diagnostic observations must be presented explicitly in the closeout decision, not counted as executable verdicts.
5. **Residual work is separated from extraction.** New-design questions have minimal motivating examples and a clear decision to make later; they do not require a complete new design in 0a. Repairs, general predecessor soundness proofs, broad live-backend testing and performance work move to a referenced backlog unless needed to determine a case's meaning or oracle observation. If the intended corpus expectation itself is ambiguous, it remains an extraction blocker.
6. **Closeout is reviewable.** Validate source anchors, hashes and schema; replay the final changed corpus; review differences and exceptions; produce one concise closure report with remaining blockers or a proposed 0a-complete conclusion. Phase 0a completion alone does not pass G0 or authorize kernel implementation before 0b.

A bounded review is a planning unit, not a waiver of completeness. If a slice exceeds its budget, report remaining rows and the reason instead of treating them as covered.
