# Grand Portage core specification — G1 ratified
GP holds claims justified by current bound evidence and sound admitted rules, with statement, model, assumptions and versions explicit. Every held claim is true in every context its scope denotes. Held also equals finite reachability under admitted checker/rule instances. GP does not establish the intended meaning of a wrongly specified problem.

**Types.** A profile supplies statements, contexts, finite scope descriptions with semantics, a decidable sound scope-inclusion test and a sound declared-contradiction test. A claim is statement+scope. Immutable warrant records include checked receipts, bound Lean proofs, derivations with premise-warrant IDs, citations, assertions and absent attempts. Bindings cover statement/scope/model data, input digests and checker/rule identities and versions. The kernel compares identities; adapters bind bytes/digests under explicit contracts.

**Held and completeness.** Resolve the final event set using explicit numeric current versions and targeted retractions/supersession. Held is the least fixpoint of active, exactly current, successful admitted evidence and sound derivations over a declared finite domain of claim keys, narrowing requests and rule instances. Particular premise-warrant IDs determine support dependencies. A failed retry does not revoke independent success. Retraction removes the targeted support and its dependents while independent support survives. Cycles cannot seed authority. Every reachable claim in that finite domain is held; arbitrary fuel truncation is insufficient.

**K1–K6.** K1 admits only validated receipts/proofs from sound registered contracts. K2 narrows universal scope through the sound inclusion test and never changes the statement's objects. K3 applies registered sound rules with live premises and checked side conditions. K4 rejects mismatched bindings. K5 excludes citation/assertion/absent evidence from authority. K6 uses a typed append-only log, total deterministic resolution and event-set order independence. Exact duplicate events are idempotent; conflicting identities and tied current-version bindings cannot acquire an arrival-order winner. Unsupported transports remain obligations.

**Inconsistency.** Compute the complete checked held closure. Contradictory statements at proved inhabited overlap freeze release/promotion separately and report their supports. Unknown overlap is unresolved. There is no unrestricted explosion rule.

**Semantic boundary.** A profile is a fixed-signature sentence/model/satisfaction fragment. Means is truth across the scope's models; it becomes theory consequence when that scope is Mod(T). Inclusion and contradiction tests are sufficient sound tests, not complete decision procedures. Selected roots, points, finite universes and regions belong in statements/models; ambient field assumptions and characteristic belong in scopes. A08b's identity requires Ring, and its emptiness consequence also requires Nontrivial; general ideal checks require CommRing.

**Ratified design.** Receipts are default working currency; durable Lean proofs are upgrades through the same exact binding interface. Check declaration types, axioms and input identity; generated native-result axioms are excluded from ordinary theorem warrants. Imitate MathEvidence contracts and qualify components individually. Use pinned Mathlib vocabulary through a separate binding layer. Native LRAT remains an admission candidate with explicit runtime/compiler assumptions. G1 is closed by Will's documented 52/67 coverage exception; no numerical 80% pass or package admission is asserted.
