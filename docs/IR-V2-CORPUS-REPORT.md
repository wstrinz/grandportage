the loaded authority state, recovers nonlocal coverage/member records, identifies

a stale receipt is retained and identified as stale. REIFIED means a recovered
data relationship, not a proved Python-to-Lean decoder. The lost set evaluates
the conditional transport gate profile at the node's stated claim kind; it is
independent of complete tree reconstruction and is never semantic irrecoverability.
